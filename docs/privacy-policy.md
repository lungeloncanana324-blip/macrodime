# MacroDime privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

MacroDime is a nutrition and food budgeting app for iPhone and Android. This
policy covers both, and describes what the app does with your information. It is
short because the app does very little with it.

## What MacroDime stores

Everything you enter stays on your device, in the app's own database:

- your body metrics (height, weight, age, sex)
- your goal, activity level, food budget and dietary preferences
- the meals you plan and the ingredients in them
- progress measurements and any progress photos you choose to add

Progress photos are copied into the app's own storage when you pick them from
your photo library. MacroDime reads only the photo you select. On Android the
photo is chosen through the system photo picker, so the app has no permission to
browse your photos at all, and the stored copy is re-encoded without its
location and camera details.

## What MacroDime sends

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no
network connection of any kind. No data is transmitted to us or to anyone else,
because the app has nowhere to send it. The Android app does not even hold the
internet permission.

Food prices are built into the app and change only when the app is updated.
Looking at a price never sends anything anywhere.

## Third parties

None. MacroDime includes no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting
software. Apple and Google may collect crash and diagnostic information about
apps installed through their stores, under their own privacy policies and your
phone's settings. That is collected by them, not by MacroDime, and it does not
include anything you enter in the app.

## Backups and new phones

On Android, MacroDime's data is excluded from Android's cloud backup to Google
Drive, so nothing you enter is copied to a server. When you move to a new phone
with Android's device-to-device transfer (by cable or direct Wi-Fi), your data is
copied straight from the old phone to the new one. On iPhone, your data is part
of your device backup in the usual way, under your Apple account.

## Your control over your data

Settings, then "Delete All My Data", permanently removes every record the app
holds, including progress photos. Uninstalling the app has the same effect.

## Health information

MacroDime estimates energy needs with published equations (Mifflin-St Jeor for
basal metabolic rate, standard activity multipliers) and prescribes a calorie
target. These are population averages. They are not medical advice, they are not
a diagnosis, and they are not a treatment. MacroDime does not transmit any health
information anywhere, so no health information is shared with us.

## Children

MacroDime is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It does not knowingly hold
information about children, and the app refuses to prescribe a calorie target for
anyone under 18.

## Notes for whoever hosts this

This file is the policy text, not a hosted page. Apple requires a live URL in App
Store Connect, and Google Play requires one in Play Console for every app,
including one that collects no data. The cheapest way to satisfy that is to publish this file through
GitHub Pages on the repo it already lives in: Settings, Pages, deploy from the
`master` branch, and use the `docs/` folder. The resulting URL looks like
`https://lungeloncanana324-blip.github.io/macrodime/privacy-policy.html`, after
renaming this file to `.html` or adding a Markdown-capable theme.
