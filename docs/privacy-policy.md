# MacroDime privacy policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

MacroDime is a nutrition and food budgeting app for iOS. This policy describes
what the app does with your information. It is short because the app does very
little with it.

## What MacroDime stores

Everything you enter stays on your device, in the app's own database:

- your body metrics (height, weight, age, sex)
- your goal, activity level, food budget and dietary preferences
- the meals you plan and the ingredients in them
- progress measurements and any progress photos you choose to add

Progress photos are copied into the app's database when you pick them from your
photo library. MacroDime reads only the photo you select.

## What MacroDime sends

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no
network connection of any kind. No data is transmitted to us or to anyone else,
because the app has nowhere to send it.

## Third parties

None. MacroDime includes no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting
software. Apple may collect crash and diagnostic information for apps installed
through the App Store under Apple's own privacy policy, and only if you have
turned that on in iOS settings.

## Your control over your data

Settings, then "Delete All My Data", permanently removes every record the app
holds, including progress photos. Deleting the app has the same effect.

## Health information

MacroDime estimates energy needs with published equations (Mifflin-St Jeor for
basal metabolic rate, standard activity multipliers) and prescribes a calorie
target. These are population averages. They are not medical advice, they are not
a diagnosis, and they are not a treatment. MacroDime does not transmit any health
information anywhere, so no health information is shared with us.

## Children

MacroDime is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It does not knowingly hold
information about children, and the app refuses to prescribe a calorie target for
anyone under 18.

## Changes
